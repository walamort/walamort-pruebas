# Walamort Pulso recorded sounds

Pitched recordings: tonejs-instruments, Nicholaus P. Brosowsky and original sample authors listed in tonejs-instruments-source-info.txt, CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Pinned source: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/tree/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5 . Files unchanged; notes between recordings retuned during playback. Piano original by Alexander Holm (Salamander Grand Piano), CC BY 3.0.

Glockenspiel: VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner, CC0. https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/tree/master/Percussion/Glock . Four original WAV recordings, retuned during playback.

Drums: Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios / Karoryfer Samples, performed by Austin McMahon. CC0 1.0. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums/tree/9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc . Extracts converted to mono MP3 (96kbps,44.1kHz,max3s,tail fade). Two source variations for each normal/accent layer. Per-file source URLs and SHA256 in manifest.json.

Ukulele and mandolin use the recorded guitar reference voice; viola uses violin recordings. These are explicitly indicated in the interface. Illustrations are AI-generated representative instrument portraits, not product photographs.
