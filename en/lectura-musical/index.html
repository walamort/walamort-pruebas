Read the staff

Choose piano, guitar, flute or violin: synthesized timbres with no downloads. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

Identify natural notes and their octaves. C4 is middle C. Complete ten questions with no time limit.

Count lines and spaces from the bottom; the clef sets the reference.

Find your way: your hand is a staff

Imagine your hand sideways with five fingers pointing right. From bottom to top, each finger represents a line and each gap represents a space. This is a memory aid, not instrument fingering.

Tap a finger or a space to see its note on the staff and hear it with your chosen instrument.

You can also use these buttons, ordered from top to bottom, to explore comfortably or with a keyboard.

Select one of the nine positions.

The hand covers the five lines and four inner spaces. Notes outside the staff use ledger lines. This explorer does not change the exercise question.

In treble clef, the lines from bottom to top are E4, G4, B4, D5 and F5. In bass clef they are G2, B2, D3, F3 and A3. In alto clef they are F3, A3, C4, E4 and G4; the center line marks middle C. Each line or space advances one letter: C, D, E, F, G, A, B.

This exercise trains visual reading of natural notes; it does not assess duration, key signatures or instrumental performance.
