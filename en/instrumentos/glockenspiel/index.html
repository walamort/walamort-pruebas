The instrument of the week has a new learning space: listen, locate the notes and build your first phrases.

What are you hearing?

A glockenspiel has metal bars arranged chromatically in two rows. Labels here show sounding pitch. In conventional orchestral notation it sounds two octaves above written pitch; this transposition is not applied to the labels. The educational range does not copy a specific commercial model.
