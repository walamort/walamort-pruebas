From the screen to your instrument

Play the open strings first, locate the tonic and explore the scale slowly. Each fret raises pitch by one semitone. The same pitch can occur on several strings; any matching position is accepted. The challenge uses frets 0–5 and scores the first answer to each question.

Positions use sharp pitch labels; the scale list uses its own spelling, which can include double sharps. Sound is synthesized and depends on your chosen timbre. This does not model string articulation, bends, left-hand fingering or alternative tunings. Results are saved only on this device.
