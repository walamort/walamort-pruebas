    Learn from the beginning

    Finish with an initial practice routine and an idea of what to repeat.

    1. 1. Find your way around the staff

      10–15 min

      Explore lines, spaces and clefs with the musical hand. Identify five notes and explain their positions.

      Open activity →

    2. 2. Listen before answering

      10–15 min

      Try an easy melodic dictation. Sing the melody and review the notes you confused.

      Open activity →

    3. 3. Feel the beat

      10–15 min

      Listen, try the rhythm and review whether your taps were early or late. Device latency can affect results.

      Open activity →

    4. 4. Build a routine

      15–20 min

      Explore the training sessions and choose a short activity to repeat this week. Marking a session does not demonstrate mastery.

      Open activity →

    Prepare for music admission

    Finish with a list of difficulties and a plan to revisit them. Check your institution’s requirements: this path does not guarantee admission.

    1. 1. Review your starting point

      15–20 min

      Review the preparation components and choose what to strengthen based on your institution’s requirements.

      Open activity →

    2. 2. Read and keep time

      15–20 min

      Practice sight reading at a comfortable tempo. Repeat a difficult passage accurately before increasing speed.

      Open activity →

    3. 3. Write what you hear

      15–20 min

      Complete a staff dictation. Evaluate your attempt and review by bar to distinguish pitch and duration errors.

      Open activity →

    4. 4. Review your results

      10–15 min

      Check your history, identify an error pattern and choose what to repeat. Keep a history backup when changing devices.

      Open activity →

    Compose and explore

    Keep a small piece and explain one melody or harmony decision.

    1. 1. Explore a harmonic relationship

      10–15 min

      Explore a key in the circle of fifths. Compare a close relationship with a more distant one; listen where the tool supports it.

      Open activity →

    2. 2. Try chords and inversions

      15–20 min

      Compare two voicings of the same chord in the harmony laboratory. Describe the change in the highest or lowest voice.

      Open activity →

    3. 3. Develop a motif

      15–20 min

      Create a short phrase with notes and rests in the creative studio. Change only its ending and compare versions.

      Open activity →

    4. 4. Take the idea into production

      15–20 min

      Save your creative project and send it to the laboratory. Keep the original before experimenting with instruments and effects.

      Open activity →

    Produce and mix

    Finish with a mixing experiment, a reflection and files to resume it. Self-study pilot; student testing is still pending.

    1. 1. Understand the change

      20–30 min

      Follow the guide’s three practices: EQ, compression and space. Record a specific observation in the journal.

      Open activity →

    2. 2. Compare beyond volume

      15–20 min

      Use the same source for A/B. Try RMS listening compensation and describe a difference beyond level.

      Open activity →

    3. 3. Edit your own phrase

      20–30 min

      Import audio you have permission to use and create a short region montage. Check order, levels and fades before exporting.

      Open activity →

    4. 4. Save and reopen

      15–20 min

      Download the montage project, keep the original audio and verify recovery. JSON contains no sound. Listen to the exported WAV too.

      Open activity →
