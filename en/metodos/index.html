Before choosing

What is available now?

The nine individual methods and three instrument packs include English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese PDFs on Hotmart. The creative workbooks remain pilots: samples are available; full editions are still under review.

Do I need an instrument?

For instrument methods, check your model’s tuning and features. For theory and creation, you can start with the free virtual tools.

How does purchasing work?

Payment takes place outside Walamort on Hotmart. Review the product, currency, final total, delivery and refund terms there before paying. This page does not process cards.

Before choosing your tongue drum method

The individual methods use an 11-note C model without B; the pack uses the complete C major scale including B. These are different note maps. Check your instrument’s actual notes before choosing.
