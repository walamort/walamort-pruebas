Before choosing

What is available now?

The current kalimba, handpan and tongue drum packs link to existing Hotmart products. The creative workbooks are pilots: their samples are available; full editions remain under review.

Do I need an instrument?

For instrument methods, check your model’s tuning and features. For theory and creation, you can start with the free virtual tools.

How does purchasing work?

Payment takes place outside Walamort on Hotmart. Review the product, currency, final total, delivery and refund terms there before paying. This page does not process cards.
