Walamort · MIDI · prototype

Your keyboard, your practice

Connect a compatible MIDI keyboard or use this page’s keys. Explore notes or answer ten reading questions. Only pitch and octave are checked; rhythm, fingering and instrumental technique are not assessed.

Prototype: the flow is tested with simulated devices and on-screen input. Your physical keyboard has not been verified; universal compatibility is not promised.

Optional connection

Permission is requested only when you press Connect MIDI. No messages are sent to your instrument and SysEx is not requested. Device names and notes are displayed here without uploading or saving them. Support depends on your browser and device.

Disconnect stops reading input; revoke any saved permission in your browser settings.

Optional audio

MIDI does not transmit instrument sound. This page offers synthesized timbres, up to 16 voices and an eight-second maximum per note to prevent stuck sound. Note off releases the voice. Pedal, pitch bend and programs are not interpreted. On-screen keys play short notes.

Reading and keyboard

Each session shuffles the five lines and four spaces. Every position appears once before one extra review question. Only pitch and octave are assessed.

Enable practice audio to compare the expected note and your original answer using the selected tone and volume. These are synthesized references, not recordings of your instrument. Listening does not count as an answer.

This session’s result is not automatically saved. Questions assess one note per answer; press Next before continuing.
