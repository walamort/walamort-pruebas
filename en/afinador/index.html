How to practice

Choose a comfortable note, listen to the reference, then enable the microphone. Use headphones so the reference sound does not enter the microphone. To compare another note, stop input and change the target. One semitone equals 100 cents: a negative value means lower than the reference, and a positive value means higher.

Reading limits

Works with one note at approximately 40–1200 Hz. Noise, chords, percussion, dominant harmonics, reverberation or fast changes may cause wrong readings or octave confusion. Stability and percentages are guidance, not a complete evaluation of voice or instrumental ability.
