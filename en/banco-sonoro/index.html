Provenance, licenses and limits

Source of this instrument ·

Nicholaus P. Brosowsky’s tonejs-instruments collection: VSCO2/Versilian Studios, Karoryfer Samples, University of Iowa, quartertone and flcellogrl depending on instrument. FluidR3 GM variants prerendered by Benjamin Gleitzman’s midi-js-soundfonts project. These are not Walamort recordings.

Each bank has one articulation/layer. It does not include every articulation, round-robin or separate recordings for each intensity. Tone files remain unedited; FluidR3 MP3 data was extracted from base64 bundles without audio modification. Authors do not endorse Walamort.
