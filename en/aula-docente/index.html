    The link contains the title, goal and activities. Use general descriptions: do not include student personal data. Anyone with the link can open it.

    Rhythmic reading, harmony, creation and fretboard links open exploration tools; choose their settings inside. Dictations and worksheets keep the selected code.

    This tool organizes lessons without accounts or online groups. Difficulty and duration are suggestions you can adapt to your class. Open tasks and their solutions are not protected exams.
