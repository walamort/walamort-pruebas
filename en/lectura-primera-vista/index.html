How to read the key signature

The full order of sharps is F, C, G, D, A, E, B; the order of flats is B, E, A, D, G, C, F. This activity uses up to three accidentals. They affect those notes in every octave. A natural beside the note cancels the alteration for this question.

Each question is a fresh reading. Advanced questions may include sharps, flats or naturals beside the note. Review the solution before going to the next question.

Only the session accuracy percentage is saved in your local history. Response times are guidance, without public ranking. The session pauses when you leave the tab.
